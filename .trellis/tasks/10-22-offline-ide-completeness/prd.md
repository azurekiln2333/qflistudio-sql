## Product decisions and boundaries
- Preserve all-language scope through owner evidence rather than claiming unrelated sibling delivery from this checkout.

- SQL has no existing breakpoint/stepping backend or managed ports. Capability limits stay visible in the ledger; fake controls are excluded.
